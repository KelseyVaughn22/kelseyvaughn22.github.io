About

I am a researcher at the University of Georgia’s Odum School of Ecology. My work integrates behavioral ecology, experimental biology, and long-term ecological data to examine how organisms and ecosystems respond to environmental change.

Research

Thermal Stress and Cognition

I investigate how rising temperatures affect learning, behavior, and predator–prey interactions in aquatic organisms.

Coral Reef Ecology

I use long-term monitoring data to evaluate coral population dynamics and responses to thermal stress in the Florida Keys.

Agrivoltaics and Biodiversity

I study how ecologically informed solar development can support biodiversity, agriculture, and conservation.
